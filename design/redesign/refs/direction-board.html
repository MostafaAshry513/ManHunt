The app is not a dashboard about a game. It is a piece of match equipment: a scoreboard, a team sheet, a chalked pitch, a field log, a match report. One plate per screen. One decisive gesture. The clock is the loudest object in the product. Cyan is the field and the referee; coral is the Prey; violet are the Hunters. Flat fills, hairlines, no cards.

So the redesign removes the card system entirely and replaces it with the objects the game actually uses: a scoreboard, a team sheet, a code, a chalk rule, a match report. The brand stops being wallpaper and becomes the layout.
