Safe areas

GitHub 1280×640 · 2:1 card, no overlay — keep ~64 px margins as used.

X 1500×500 · avatar overlaps bottom-left (~160 px wide); keep text right of x≈190 and away from the bottom 120 px on the left.

YouTube 2560×1440 · text must sit inside the centred 1546×423 safe area; the outer field is safe to crop.

Discord 960×540 · 16:9, no overlay.

Honesty + brand rules

Every banner carries the honest line “free APK · Android 8+ · not on Play Store” where it fits; nothing promises Play Store, safety, reviews or fixed hunt lengths.

Lockup is inlined verbatim from brand/identity/final/lockup/ — never retyped, re-tracked or recoloured. Cyan is never a team colour; coral is Prey; violet is Hunters.

Flat fills only. Animated variants are for posts/embeds — the platform header uploads above are static surfaces.
