Before you play

The street is not the game.

ManHunt is played on public ground. The server can own the clock; it can’t keep you safe.

01

Stay in public.

Parks, pavements and well-lit streets. Never enter private property, gardens or anywhere you would not walk alone at night.

02

Respect non-players.

Do not chase, crowd or film people who did not opt in. If someone asks you to leave, leave.

03

Keep phones on.

Location is only sent while a hunt runs. Battery low or signal gone means the field cannot see you.

Field manual / read once

How a
night runs.

PREYhide · confirm tag
HUNTERSread · chase · tag
01

Pick a side.

Prey hide. Hunters follow the oldest report on the map — never the live position. The server sets both at the start.

02

One clock.

The match has a fixed duration and one shrinking zone. Both teams see the same clock. Nobody can pause it.

03

Tag in person.

When a hunter is close enough to touch a prey, they tap Tag. The prey confirms or disputes. The server decides.
