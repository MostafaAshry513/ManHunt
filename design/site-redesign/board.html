01 · the one idea

Nightfixture.

The site is the matchday programme for a borrowed field: a poster for tonight's game, and the printed sheet that explains the sport. A borrowed pitch needs no stadium — it needs a fixture, a field map, two sides, a clock and a set of rules. So the page is laid out like that: one huge slogan over real ground, a programme rail down the margin, an annotated field plate, a rules ledger and a field pass. The brand is the structure, not the wallpaper. It is built from the same props as the app redesign: the board, the kit tags, the chalk rules, the Open Zone.

01 · the gestureA poster over real ground.

The type is the poster

Slogan at 140 px condensed grotesque, three lines, no accent word. It is the biggest object in the first viewport — larger than the logo, the nav and the CTA.

The field is real

OpenStreetMap tiles, filtered through the app's exact Deep Teal matrix. Zone ring, coral Prey, violet Hunters, a chalk trail, a server clock. It bleeds off the frame — the field is bigger than the screen.

One loud thing

Everything else is hairlines, space and measured type. No gradients, no shadows, no glass, no neon HUD. The boldness budget is spent once.

The conversion is honest

Free APK · version · size · Android 8+ · not on Play Store, directly under the primary action. One cyan fill per viewport.

Cyan is never a team and coral/violet are never the field. The one rule that keeps a location game legible.

One hot cyan surface per viewport: the hero CTA, later the pass CTA. Nav buttons are outlines for this reason.

Team colour is never the only cue. Every Prey/Hunters mark pairs colour with a glyph and a name.

Flat fills only. Depth comes from overlap, hairlines and plate tone steps — zero box-shadows.
