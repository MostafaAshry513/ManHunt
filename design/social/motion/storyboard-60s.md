## Claims / accuracy audit

- **No duration copy anywhere.** The clock is visual only; the hunt length is host-set (default 60 min).
- **Rules are correct:** a tag eliminates **one** prey; the hunt ends when every prey is tagged or the clock runs out — hence `Tagged. They're out.` (not "round over"). Tag step says *touch*, matching the in-person mechanic; no contradiction with the prey-confirm flow.
- **Tracking wording is honest:** `Hunters see your last report.` — latest reported positions, with an optional trail if the room enables it. No "live map", no "private", no "encrypted".
- No player counts, scores, testimonials, fake urgency, or "safe / survive / protect / anywhere".
- Install honesty: free APK, Android 8+, not on Play Store, bare-IP URL.
- No real screenshots, photos, people, map tiles or OSM data → no OSM attribution needed.
- Every beat line ≤ 7 words; no one-word captions; no exclamation marks or emoji; sentence case with full stops.
