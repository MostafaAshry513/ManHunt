## 5. Claims / disclosure guardrails

- **No duration claims.** The clock is shown as real capture data; the hunt length is host-set (default 60 min). Never write "40 minutes", "always 60", or "the round is 18:48".
- **Tracking is described honestly:** "latest reports", never "live map", "real-time", "private", "encrypted", "untraceable".
- **No safety or legal promises:** no safe / survive / protect / anywhere / trespass copy.
- **No fake social proof:** no player counts, wins, reviews, testimonials, ratings, urgency.
- **Tag rules:** one prey is out per confirmed tag; the match ends on the clock or when every prey is tagged. Do not say "round over" after one tag.
- **Install honesty:** free APK, Android 8+, not on Google Play, bare-IP link. Keep the "not on Play Store" line wherever the install CTA appears.
- **OSM attribution:** any frame showing map tiles keeps `© OpenStreetMap contributors`; do not crop it out.
- **Provisional copy:** mark every post draft "copy provisional — owner approval pending" until approved.
- Arabic is MSA and not yet RTL-rendered; an Arabic cut needs a layout pass + native review.
- The captures show debug-build test match data (usernames Yasmin/Leila/Omar/Tariq/Sara, room VMJYBU). They are app test data, not real people or endorsements.
