LAUNCHER ICON STUDY — 01
NIGHT SHIFT / ADAPTIVE 108dp
NOT APPLIED YET

A new launcher icon for a real-world hide-and-seek game. The app pages were rebuilt from zero around bitumen, bone, flare, Bricolage and the report dot; the icon has to belong to that system without becoming a tiny app screen. These are eight starts, not one safe answer.

PRODUCT
A live two-team hide-and-seek game played on real streets.
CHARACTER
Tense, exact, a little playful. Not tactical, not toy.
WORLD
Night streets, printed tickets, folded field maps, one green report point.
MOMENT
The moment a report lands: one dot, old rings, a place you were.
SIGNATURE
One lime point in or beside the mark. Every direction keeps it.

What the old icon was doing

  • A ring and a coral dot. It reads as a loading wheel or a generic radar, not a street game.
  • Cyan and coral. Neither colour exists in NIGHT SHIFT anymore.
  • Thin geometry. The ring breaks down at 48 px and disappears at 32 px.
  • No monochrome story. Android 13 themed icons need a shape, not a colour wheel.

What this study optimises for

  • Silhouette first. Readable at 48 px and still recognisable at 32 px.
  • One idea per mark. Ticket, fold, signal, cut, block, trace. No five-element mascot.
  • Adaptive-safe. All marks sit inside the 66dp circle; tested in circle and squircle.
  • One lime point. The game's report dot is the only colour event besides flare.
