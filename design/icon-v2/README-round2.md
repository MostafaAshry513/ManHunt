# ManHunt launcher icon - study 02

Round one was eight marks. None of them had a player in it. This round puts the game back into the icon: a hider, a hound, a searchlight, a runner, a patch, an eye, an M, a claw.

Full sheet: [round2-study-sheet.png](round2-study-sheet.png)
Individual 512 PNGs: [samples2/](samples2/)
Source file: [round2.html](round2.html) + [build_round2.py](build_round2.py)

Nothing is applied to the app yet.

## Direction

- **Product:** a live two-team hide-and-seek game played on real streets.
- **Character:** alive, tense, a little dangerous. A game people play, not a dashboard they use.
- **World:** night streets, a wall to hide behind, a torch, a crew patch.
- **Moment:** the second before you are found, or the second before you find someone.
- **Signature:** one lime report point on every direction.

## Why round one failed

- All eight were marks: ticket, ring, fold, grid, monogram. No player, no story, no reason to pick one up.
- Too polite: the palette was right; the forms were all thin, centred, logo-exercise geometry.
- Same silhouette family: most read as a map or a scanner, not a hunt.
- No character: the app pages have a voice; the icons had a grid.

## Eight directions

| # | Name | One line | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Peek** | A hider half behind a flare wall, one eye | Most human; hide-and-seek reads instantly |
| 02 | **Hound** | A blunt hound head with a dark muzzle | Character without a mascot costume |
| 03 | **Flash** | A hand searchlight cutting the night | A tool from the hunt, not a signal |
| 04 | **Run** | A body mid-stride over a flare ground line | Most kinetic |
| 05 | **Patch** | A crew patch with an M bolt | Most merch-like; apparel seed |
| 06 | **Watch** | An eye: the field watches back | Most graphic; shortcut for "hunted" |
| 07 | **Stencil** | A flare plate with the M cut through it | Most ownable brand stamp |
| 08 | **Claw** | Three flare cuts across a bone plate | Most aggressive; hunter side |

## What survives the launcher

Tested in circle and squircle, plus a light monochrome themed-icon treatment at 48 px.

- **Peek** - a player under a wall. At 24 px it is still a head and a wall.
- **Hound** - a face with two ears. At 24 px the eyes and muzzle still read.
- **Flash** - a tool with a beam and a target dot. At 24 px the diagonal still reads.

Picks from this round: **Peek** for the most human game icon, **Hound** for character, **Flash** for a tool-first hunt icon. **Watch** is the strongest alternate if the icon should be graphic rather than a character.

## Size / mask tests

- Dark ground at 512, 48, 32 and 24 px
- Circle and squircle adaptive masks for the shortlist
- Monochrome layer on bone for the shortlist
- All marks inside the 66 dp safe zone
- One accent (flare) plus the single lime report point; no gradients, glows or shadows
