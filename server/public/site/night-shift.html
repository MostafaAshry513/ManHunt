Rules that hold.

The rules are the product. These are not settings; they are the game.

01

The clock is the server’s.

It starts at the room, not on a phone. Closing the app does not pause the hunt.

02

Locations are reports.

Hunters see the last report and its age. Nobody sees a live cursor for the other team.

03

Tags are confirmed.

Prey confirms or disputes. A dispute goes to the host or server; it never disappears.

04

The zone shrinks.

Every round ends. The field tightens. Running out of space is part of the game.
